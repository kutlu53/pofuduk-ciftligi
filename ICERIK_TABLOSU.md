## Tema 1: School Life
Alt temalar: Greetings and introductions at school; People and places at school; Months of the year; Ages and birthday celebrations; National days  
Sayfa: https://tymm.meb.gov.tr/ingilizce-dersi-temel-egitim/unite/765

**Sözcükler**

- *people*: a classmate, a librarian, a school nurse, a headteacher, a caretaker
- *places*: an art room, a music room, a meeting room, an office, a sports field, a corridor, a gym
- *birthday*: birthday, a birthday party, a candle, a cake, a balloon, a gift, summer holiday, a surprise
- *months*: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December
- *verbs*: to prepare, to decorate, to bring, to play the guitar, to write a song, to ask, to find, to run, to help

**Kalıplar**

- Who's this? — Bu kim? (L, S)
- This is Mr. Blue. — Bu Bay Blue. (R, W)
- Where is Daphne? She's in the library. — Daphne nerede? Kütüphanede. (L, R)
- What month is it now? It's September. — Şimdi hangi ay? Eylül. (L, S, R)
- How old are you? I'm 8 years old. — Kaç yaşındasın? 8 yaşındayım. (L, S, W)
- When's your birthday? It's in July. — Doğum günün ne zaman? Temmuzda. (L, S, R)
- Whose birthday is in October? — Kimin doğum günü ekimde? (L, R)
- Is there a nurse at school? — Okulda hemşire var mı? (R, G)
